Spice Math

The paprika is decor, and the recipe's teaspoons were never meant for nine people.

Boundary: illustrative arithmetic only The freshness windows are a pantry rule people cite - ground 2 to 3 years, whole 3 to 4, dried herbs 1 to 3 - and flavor fade is the only thing at stake. This page never makes a food-safety call. The rest is your own arithmetic.

The age truth

Ground spices fade out of the cited window after about 2 to 3 years, whole after 3 to 4, dried herbs after 1 to 3. Past it, if it smells like nothing, it tastes like nothing.

The scale truth

Scaling 2.0 teaspoons from 4 servings to 9 asks 4.5 teaspoons - 1 tablespoon plus 1.5 teaspoons. Doubling the chili and forgetting the cumin is a choice, not a mistake.

The cabinet truth

Eighteen jars at $4.50 each is $81.00 of powder. The rack was the cheap part.
